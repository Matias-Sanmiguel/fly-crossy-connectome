MaleCNS V2 perspective camera fix

Fixes two independent issues:
1. The prior pytest guards searched raw source text and falsely matched words in docstrings.
2. More importantly, the triangle rasterizer rejected negative-screen-area triangles even
   though barycentric weights were already normalized by signed area. This caused most lane
   ground surfaces to disappear while some 3-D box faces still rendered.

The gate now requires actual pixels of road/rail/river base colors, preventing this false PASS.

Extract from the REPOSITORY ROOT, not from the python subdirectory.
